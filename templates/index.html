      📖 Academic Rubric & Viva Reference Guide (5 Criteria × 0–4 Marks = 20 Marks Total)
      1. Purpose (Problem, Gap, Aim) [0–4]: Evaluates whether the research problem is stated, an explicit gap in prior literature is identified, and the specific aim of the paper is clear.
      2. Method (Approach, Data, Setup) [0–4]: Evaluates whether the algorithm, model architecture, benchmark datasets, and evaluation methodology are explicitly detailed.
      3. Results (Findings, Numbers, Comparison) [0–4]: Evaluates concrete quantitative metrics (e.g. accuracy, speedup, p-values) and direct comparisons against baseline systems.
      4. Conclusion (Contribution, Implications, Limits) [0–4]: Evaluates stated contributions, practical/theoretical significance, and acknowledged limitations or future directions.
      5. Clarity (Readable, Organised, ~120–250 Words) [0–4]: Evaluates academic flow (Context → Problem → Method → Result → Impact), readability, and compliance with the 120–250 word standard.
